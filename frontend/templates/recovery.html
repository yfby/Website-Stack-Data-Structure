WHY THIS ORDER?

Undo should feel like undo.

When you remove A, then B, then C, the stack looks like this:

C · top / nextBA · bottom

Restoring takes C first, then B, then A. Peeking at the next task never removes it, and the order survives a page refresh because the list is saved in SQLite.

The stack is the site's real recovery logic, not a visual trick.
